    Terra Analog · Planetary Terrain Analysis

    Research prototype. Similarity describes terrain geometry only.

    About Terra Analog

    Terra Analog compares the shape of an Earth landscape with a planetary terrain reference. Mars elevations come from the NASA Mars Global Surveyor MOLA gridded topography (MEGDR). Earth elevations come from the GeoTIFF you upload. The file is processed for the request only.

    How it compares

    Both models are brought to the same ground resolution and the same window size. The engine then compares the distributions of local relief, slope and surface roughness, and combines them into a 0–100 index.

    Limitations

    The methodology is provisional and uncalibrated. A high index means similar terrain geometry at the compared scale. It is not a probability and says nothing about habitability, safety or suitability for exploration.
